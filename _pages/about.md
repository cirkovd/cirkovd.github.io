I am currently an Assistant Professor in the <a href="https://www.marquette.edu/mathematical-and-statistical-sciences/" target="_blank">Department of Mathematical and Statistical Sciences</a> at Marquette University. My current research interests include random networks and extreme value theory. In particular, I am interested in the scale-free behavior of degree distributions, as well as temporal dynamics in random networks. 

I received a Ph.D. in Statistics from Texas A&M University in 2025. I also obtained a Master's degree in Statistics and Bachelor's degree in Mathematics & Statistics from Miami University in 2020.
